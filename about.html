About Me

Professional Biography

Committed to Healthcare Excellence & Pharmacy Education

Dr. Ochieng Edwine William is a dedicated Clinical Pharmacist, Pharmacy Trainer, and academic professional with over five years of experience in healthcare, pharmaceutical practice, and technical education. He currently serves as the Deputy Course Coordinator and Head of Section for Pharmaceutics, Industrial Pharmacy and Pharmacy Practice at The Mawego National Polytechnic, where he is committed to developing competent healthcare professionals through quality teaching, mentorship, and competency-based training.

Throughout his career, Dr. Ochieng has worked in hospitals, community pharmacies, and academic institutions, gaining extensive experience in patient care, medication therapy management, pharmaceutical operations, drug supply management, and clinical pharmacy practice. His professional journey reflects a strong commitment to evidence-based healthcare, patient safety, and continuous improvement in pharmaceutical services.

Beyond clinical practice, he actively contributes to pharmacy education, curriculum development, research supervision, and academic leadership. His goal is to bridge the gap between healthcare practice and education by mentoring future pharmacists while promoting excellence, innovation, and ethical professional practice.
